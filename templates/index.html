Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes — completely free. No ads, no watermark, no daily limit.

Which qualities are supported?

4K (2160p), 2K (1440p), 1080p, 720p, 360p and MP3 (192kbps audio only). If the source video doesn't have 4K, the closest best quality is picked automatically.

Can I download whole playlists?

This version downloads one video at a time. Playlist support is coming in the next update.

Where do the files go / how long are they stored?

Files are kept on the server only for a short time and auto-deleted. Download them to your own device right away. Please only download your own videos, content you have permission for, or Creative Commons videos.
